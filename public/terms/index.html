Terms, delivery and returns

Who sells to you

The merchant of record for every merch order is DATRO CONSORTIUM LIMITED, a company registered in England and Wales. DATRO runs this shop pro bono, on behalf of the Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr heritage project. It sells at cost and not for profit; any surplus goes to the project.

Order requests and payment

This shop currently accepts order requests only. Submitting the basket form does not create an accepted sale, take payment, reserve stock or authorise production. We will review the request and, where the item can be supplied, provide a quotation in pounds sterling (GBP), including the proposed delivery cost and any product options. A sale is formed only after the quotation and payment arrangements have been confirmed. This website does not collect or store card details and must not be used to send payment-card information.

No payment is taken when you submit a request. Do not send money until you have received and accepted the written quotation and payment instructions.

Printing and delivery

Production and dispatch are not arranged when an order request is submitted. If the request can be fulfilled, the quotation will explain the proposed production, delivery cost and estimated times. No production or dispatch is promised until the quotation is accepted and payment is confirmed.

Returns and faults

Items are made to order, so we can't take returns for a change of mind (the right to cancel doesn't apply to personalised or made-to-order goods under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013). If an item arrives damaged, misprinted or faulty, tell us within 30 days with a photo and we'll arrange a free reprint or a full refund. Your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 are not affected.

Contact

Questions about an order: info@bucklerfamily.estate. Please include your order number.

The designs

All designs were made for this shop and are © DATRO CONSORTIUM LIMITED for the Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr project. Pictures of the farm are real photographs from the farm’s own album, restored (upscaled, contrast, toning, sky removal) without changing what they show. The Peerless Jim photograph is a public-domain archive photograph (Bain News Service, Library of Congress). The other designs are typography.
